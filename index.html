WELCOME TO YOUR INSTRUMENT

You make the music.

Start the camera or play with your keyboard. Nothing plays automatically.

Air drums

Lift your hand above the dotted line, then strike down into a pad. Lift to rearm. With keys, use A S D F. You can also tap the pads.

Melody

Touch thumb to index finger to hold a note. Move sideways to choose pitch and up to brighten it. Open your fingers to release. With a mouse or touch, hold and drag on the stage. Keys A S D F G H J play the scale.

Your loop

Record a layer gives you four count-in beats, then records eight beats. It repeats automatically. Add up to four layers, mute them individually, and export the mix as a WAV file.

Your space

Set your playing area by holding your hand at two comfortable corners. Calibration and loops save locally. Camera model files download when first needed.
